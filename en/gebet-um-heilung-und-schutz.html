Prayer for Healing and Protection

Prayer for Healing and Protection

Lord Jesus Christ, we thank You for Your love for us. Shelter us all in Your wounds and grant us Your salvation. Lord, we ask You, mark us with Your seal and Mary’s seal, that we may be preserved from all attacks of the Evil One and from all harm. From curses, imprecations, possession, melancholy, depression and fear – deliver us, O Lord!

You tell us again and again: Do not be afraid, I am with you. Therefore we need have no fear.

We ask You, O Lord, through the intercession of the Most Blessed Virgin Mary, grant us deliverance from every curse and imprecation and gladden us at all times with Your peace. Lord Jesus Christ, You are full of love for us human beings and You have all power in heaven and on earth. Touch us with Your healing love. Heal everything in our hearts, in our souls, that is injured and wounded. Heal all the memories of our lives that hurt us. Heal all the wounds that other people have inflicted on us. Heal everything that is injured and wounded. Lay Your pierced, healing hands on all our wounds of soul and body, until they are completely healed. Lord Jesus Christ, You also know all our bodily illnesses. Touch them with Your healing power, with Your healing warmth, and make whole all our organs that are ill. For You are the Savior of the whole world!

Father in heaven, in the name of Jesus, Your Son, we ask You, grant us Your fatherly love in abundant measure, and let us feel this love now also in our hearts. Amen.

This spontaneous prayer by Fr. Klaus Müller (1946–2001) was written down afterwards. His life was that of a saintly priest. Many miracles have occurred through his prayer and intercession. He had many charisms, which he bestowed abundantly on those who sought help. For protection from all evil and for a future secure in God, from 1994 he marked on the forehead with the seal of God all people who asked him to.
